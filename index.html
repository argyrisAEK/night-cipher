Night Cipher

Ten ways to hide a message in one picture. Can you spot the pattern?

How to play

Every picture in Night Cipher hides a secret word. Your job is to spot the pattern and read it.

  1. Study the picture. The letters are hidden in something you can count or compare, like lit windows, bar heights, petals or dots. Look for a pattern rather than written text.
  2. Type your guess on the right. You get 2–3 tries, and a guess counts when it is at least 90% similar. Stuck? A hint costs 20 points.
  3. Learn the trick. When the round ends, press “How was it hidden?” to see the method with the letters highlighted.

Four ways to play

  • 📅 Daily One puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Build a streak.
  • 🎓 Learn Ten levels, each with a different hiding method.
  • ⚔ Duel vs AI 💰 An AI hides your message in a picture. Can you and an AI seeker read it back?
  • 🕵 You hide, AI seeks 💰 Pick a word and a scheme and see whether an AI can crack it.

💰 = uses your Pollen. Daily and Learn work without signing in; only the 💰 buttons need the sign-in button at the top right.
