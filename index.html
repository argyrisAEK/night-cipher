Night Cipher

Hidden in plain sight.

A visual steganography game and learning lab : solve hidden messages yourself or watch AI models hide and decode them.

How to play

Night Cipher is both a puzzle game and a hands-on learning lab. Decode visual messages, practise the ciphers, experiment with pixel-level hiding, or investigate how reliably AI models can hide and recover a secret.

Play a round

  1. Read the image, not the text. Look for a visual pattern: count windows, compare heights, follow star sequences, inspect shapes or notice colour changes. Each pattern represents letters.
  2. Make your guess. In Daily and Learn, enter the hidden message and use hints when you need them. Daily gives you a shared daily puzzle; Learn guides you through ten levels and ten different ciphers.
  3. Reveal and understand. After the round, choose How was it hidden? to see the encoding explained and the relevant parts of the image annotated.

Game modes

  • 📅 Daily — solve the daily puzzle, build your streak and compare your result with the same daily challenge.
  • 🎓 Learn — work through ten guided levels, practise each deterministic visual cipher and track your progress.
  • ⚔ AI vs AI Duel 💰 — choose a secret. The AI Hider embeds it in an image; a blind AI Seeker receives the image without the answer or hiding instructions and tries to decode it.
  • 🕵 You hide, AI seeks 💰 — choose your own word and one of the ten ciphers, then test whether a vision model can recover it.

Explore the learning lab

  • 🧩 Interactive Sandbox — experiment with two kinds of encoding. In Pixel-level LSB, hide and recover UTF-8 text in an image’s RGB least-significant bits, inspect pixels, edit individual channel bits and export the result as PNG. In Structural schemes, change the message and cipher and inspect the live image plus its letter-by-letter encoding.
  • 🧭 Interactive Cipher Walkthrough — learn all ten training ciphers step by step: observe the image, identify its visual rule, convert the pattern into letters and verify the decoded message.

Put AI to the test

  • 🧪 Research Mode 💰 — compare Hider and Seeker models with recovery metrics, a model leaderboard and matchup matrix. Run an Independent Seeker check to compare a second model’s blind answer with the primary Seeker.
  • 🛡 Robustness tests 💰 — test whether a message survives different image encodings and controlled degradation such as JPEG compression, resizing, blur, noise and cropping.
  • 🔍 Evidence & diagnostics — inspect experiment outcomes, filter the local evidence log by model or result, and export records as CSV or JSON. Optional stealth judging rates how innocent an image appears.

How LSB encoding works
  1. Each red, green and blue channel is an 8-bit number from 0 to 255.
  2. The least-significant bit (LSB) is the final binary digit. Changing it alters a channel value by at most 1.
  3. This lab writes one message bit into each RGB channel, in row-major pixel order. Alpha values are left untouched.
  4. A small header identifies this lab's format and stores the UTF-8 message length. The header consumes 7 bytes of capacity.

Important: export and share the result as PNG. JPEG is lossy and may change the embedded bits. This simple educational encoder is not encrypted, authenticated, or designed to resist steganalysis.
